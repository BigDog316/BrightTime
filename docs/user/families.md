## Finding activities

You don't need an account to search.

**On the website**, start from the home page or **Search**:
- Type what you're looking for, such as *robotics*, *swim* or *homework help*. Small spelling
  mistakes are forgiven.
- Narrow the results by **age**, **kind of listing** (programs, events, drop-in resources, jobs
  and internships), **neighbourhood**, **highest price** or **free only**.
- Sort by **best match**, **starts soonest**, **nearest** (once you've chosen a neighbourhood) or
  **newest**.
- Show the results as a **list** or a **map** of the county's neighbourhoods.

Every filter is in the page's address, so you can bookmark a search or send it to someone. The
site also has **collections** (activities a network organiser has grouped, such as *Summer
camps*), a page for each **neighbourhood** with what's on there this week, and a page for each
**organisation**.

**In the app**, tap the search box, then the filters. The app adds a few more filters: **drop in,
no sign-up**, **food provided** and **transport provided**. **Near me** uses your phone's
location once to pick the closest neighbourhood. Your location isn't kept.

**A listing's page** tells you what it is, who it's for (ages and grades), when it meets (in local
time), where, how much it costs, which languages are spoken, and access details such as
wheelchair access, meals and transport. Under **How to join** it says what to do:
- *Just come along*, for drop-in activities;
- register on the organisation's own website;
- contact the organisation; or
- *Registration through BrightTime opens soon*, for programs that will take applications here.
  Until then, contact the organisation to save a place.

Some places show only the neighbourhood, not the street address, for example a program held in a
shelter. The exact address is shared once you register.

## Saving activities and reminders

- **On the website**, tap **Save** on any listing. Saved activities are kept **in this browser
  only**, so anyone else using it can see them. The **Saved** page groups them by deadline, and
  **Add reminders to your calendar** downloads a calendar file that reminds you a day before each
  deadline and an hour before each session. BrightTime doesn't keep your contact details for this.
- **In the app**, sign in to save. Saved activities follow your account to any phone or tablet,
  and the app keeps your last list for when you're offline. On a listing you can also ask to be
  reminded **a day before the deadline** or **an hour before it starts**.

## Your feed, interests and area (app)

Once you're signed in, the app's home page is **Picked for you**. Each card says why it was
shown, for example *matches 2 of your interests* or *starts in 3 days*.

- **Your interests** (under **Account**) are put first in your feed. They are the only
  preference of yours the feed uses, and you can change them whenever you like.
- **Your area** is the neighbourhood your feed searches from. Choose one, or tap **Use my
  location** to pick the closest. BrightTime never keeps your address or where you are.

## Notifications (app)

The **Inbox** holds every notification BrightTime sends you. Under **Account → Notifications**
you choose, for each kind (session reminders, deadline reminders, changes to saved activities,
new messages), whether it reaches you **in your inbox**, **on your phone** or **by email**.

**Quiet hours** stop your phone showing notifications between two times you choose. They still
go to your inbox.

Email notifications aren't sent yet: for now, choose the inbox or your phone.

## Your account

**Creating an account.** In the app, tap **Create an account**, or use the website's sign-in
page:
1. Say the month and year you were born. BrightTime never asks for the day. Adults' accounts
   don't keep it at all.
2. Give your email address. We send a link to choose a password.
3. Open the link, choose a password, and sign in.

Someone under 13 can't make an account. A parent or guardian adds them to their own account
instead (see below).

**Signing in.** Use a **passkey** if your phone or computer has one, or your email address and
password. Your county may also offer sign-in with another account, such as Google.

**Passkeys** let you sign in with your phone's fingerprint, face or screen lock instead of a
password. Add one under **Account → Passkeys** in the app, or **Sign-in and security** on the
website. You can remove any passkey there too.

**Forgot your password?** Use **Forgot your password?** on the sign-in page. We email a link to
choose a new one. For your safety, the page says the same thing whether or not the address has
an account.

**Changing your password** or your sign-in methods on the website asks you to confirm who you are
again if you last did so more than a few minutes ago.

**Sign out on all devices**, under **Account** in the app, ends your sessions everywhere, for
example after losing a phone.

**Terms of use.** You can read the terms at any time on the website's **Terms** page or in the
app. Agreeing to them is needed before you sign up for programs yourself.

## Children's profiles and consent (app)

Children under 13 don't have accounts of their own. You add them to yours, under **Your
family** in the app.

**Adding a child.**
1. Tap **Add a child**.
2. Give their **first name or nickname**, the **month and year they were born**, and **your
   relationship** to them. BrightTime keeps nothing else: no last name, no birthday.
3. We email you a link. **Open it to give your consent.** Until then the profile shows as
   *Waiting for your consent* and can't be used.

The link opens in the app if it's installed, or on the website, where you sign in as yourself to
confirm.

**Each child's page** shows their consents, one per thing they cover:

| Consent | Lets your child… |
|---|---|
| Using BrightTime | use the platform at all |
| Signing up for programs | be signed up for programs |
| Messages from programs | receive messages from programs |
| Taking part in groups | take part in group spaces |
| Uploading photos of their work | add photos of what they make |
| Showing their work publicly | have their work shown publicly |
| Attendance photos | appear in attendance photos |
| Using their location | use location features |
| Sharing with their school district | have information shared with their school district |
| Use in research | be included in research |

To give one, tap **Read and give consent**. You see the exact words you're agreeing to, and
BrightTime records that you agreed to those words. **Withdraw consent** takes it back at any time.

Some consents disclose more about a child, and need a **signed form** instead of an email. The
app says so. Ask the program or your network organiser for the form. Once they've checked it,
the consent shows as given.

**Stop managing this profile** ends every consent you gave for that child.

Your child's account can do only what you've consented to. Their profile's state is one of:
*Ready to use*, *Waiting for your consent*, *Paused until you give consent*, or *Has their own
sign-in*.

## Giving a child their own sign-in at 13

When a child on your account turns 13, they can have their own sign-in. On their page, choose
**Give them their own sign-in** and enter their email address. We email them a link to choose a
password; then they sign in themselves. You can still manage their consents.

## Your household (app)

Under **Account**, choose **Your household**. You enter your household once, and every program you
apply to uses it.

- **About your household**: how many people live in your home, the language spoken there, and
  whether you need an interpreter.
- **People in your household**: choose **Add someone** for each person: who they are (a child, a
  young person of 13 or over, a parent or guardian, or another adult), their names, the name they
  go by, and their relationship to you. A young person's date of birth and school grade matter:
  programs that check ages see the date of birth, and nobody else does.
- **Support needs**, for a child or young person: an allergy, a medical need, a disability or
  anything else that helps a program look after them, with a note on what helps. A need is shared
  only if you tick **Programs may see this**.
- **Their own account**: link a child or young person to the account they use (yours, if this is
  you, or a child's profile on your account), so the programs they attend and the badges they earn
  go on their portfolio.
- **Contacts**: people a program can call in an emergency, or who may pick up a child.

**Remove from household** takes a person off. Programs already sharing their details stop seeing
them.

### Who can see your household

**See who can see it** lists every program you've shared your household with: the people, the
details, since when, and until when. **Stop sharing** ends a share at once; the program stops
seeing those details right away. Shares you stopped, or that ended, stay on the list so you can see
what was shared.

## Applying, paying and messages: coming soon

The parts of BrightTime that let you apply to programs through the site are built and being
tested, and the screens for families come next. When they arrive you'll be able to:

- **Choose what each organisation sees** when you apply. You share chosen family members and
  chosen details with one organisation at a time, for one program or all of them, and until a
  date you pick. Stopping a share is already in the app (above).
- **Apply** to a program by answering its form, after seeing who it's for. If your child doesn't
  meet a rule that must be met, you're told before you apply.
- **Sign waivers**, such as a photo release. A parent or guardian signs for anyone under 18, and
  you sign the exact text the organisation published.
- **Hear back**: accepted, waitlisted or declined. If a place opens up, you're offered it and can
  take it or say no.
- **Pay fees**, choosing the price level your household fits. Nobody asks your income. Card
  payments happen on the payment service's own page, never on BrightTime.
- **Check in** with a code on your phone that staff scan, and see your child's attendance.
- **Read messages** from your child's programs and their announcements.

## Your privacy

- BrightTime never keeps a child's home address or exact location.
- An organisation sees only what you share with it, only while the share lasts, and every time
  its staff open your details, it's recorded.
- No adult can be alone in a conversation with a young person on BrightTime. An admin of the
  organisation always reads along, and everyone in the conversation is told so.
- Reports that organisations give funders hide small numbers, so nobody can be picked out.
- Card details never pass through BrightTime.
