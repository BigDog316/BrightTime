# User guides

BrightTime helps a county's families and young people find things to do outside school, and
helps the organisations that run those activities manage them. Each guide below is written for
one kind of person.

| If you are… | Read |
|---|---|
| A parent or guardian | [Families](families.md) |
| 13 or over, with your own account | [Young people](young-people.md) |
| Someone who runs activities for an organisation | [Organisations](providers.md) |
| A network organiser for the county | [Network organisers](organisers.md) |

One person can be more than one of these. A parent who also coaches a team uses both the families
and the organisations guide, with the same account.

## Where BrightTime runs

- **The website**, on the county's own address (for the seed region, `kettle.localhost` in
  development). Anyone can search it without an account, and it works with JavaScript turned
  off, on a slow connection or an old phone. Organisations and network organisers use its
  **console**: the pages under *Your organisations*, *Review queue* and the other links that
  appear once you sign in.
- **The app**, for Android and iOS. It searches the same listings and adds what a phone does
  well: a personal feed, reminders, notifications, a family's consent centre, and taking
  attendance without a signal.

## Languages

Everything is offered in English, Spanish, Chinese (Simplified), Bengali, Haitian Creole and
Arabic. Choose a language at the foot of any web page, or under **Account** in the app. Arabic
reads right to left throughout.

The translations are machine drafts until native speakers have reviewed them. A listing that was
translated by machine says so.

## Words used in these guides

- **Listing** or **activity**: one thing to do: a program, an event, a drop-in resource, or a job
  or internship.
- **Organisation**: a group that runs activities. Families sometimes call them providers.
- **Site**: a place where an organisation runs activities.
- **Network organiser**: the county's own staff, who check every organisation and listing before
  it goes live.
- **Consent**: a parent's or guardian's agreement, given one thing at a time, for a child under 13
  to use part of BrightTime.
- **Share**: what a family chooses to let one organisation see about its members, and for how
  long.
